# Supporting Documents Checklist

The following documents must accompany every loan application submitted to
Hangs SACCO.

## Required for all loan products

1. Completed and signed loan application form.
2. A copy of the member's National ID or valid passport.
3. One recent passport-size photograph.
4. Statements of savings/contributions for the last 3 months.
5. Signed guarantor consent form(s), one per required guarantor.

## Additional documents by loan product

- **School Fees Loan**: an official fee structure or invoice from the
  learning institution, showing the amount due and the term/semester it
  covers.
- **Development Loan**: a short written statement of the business or
  development purpose the loan will fund (no formal business plan template
  is required).
- **Emergency Loan**: proof of the emergency where reasonably available
  (for example, a hospital bill or a death certificate). This is
  encouraged but not mandatory for the Emergency Loan to be processed.

## Notes

- Photocopies must be legible; the loan officer may request the original
  document for verification.
- Incomplete document sets will not be forwarded to the Credit Committee.

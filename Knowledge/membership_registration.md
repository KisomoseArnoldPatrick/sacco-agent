# Membership Registration

## Eligibility to join

- Applicants must be at least 18 years old.
- Applicants must complete a Membership Application Form and a Know-Your-Member
  (KYM) form, as described in `member_identification.md`.

## Registration costs

- A one-time, non-refundable registration fee of **UGX 20,000**.
- A minimum initial share capital contribution of **UGX 30,000**.

## Approval

- New membership applications are reviewed and approved by the Board of
  Directors within **7 working days** of submission.
- Once approved, the applicant is issued a Membership Number in the format
  described in `member_identification.md`.

## Becoming loan-eligible

- A new member does not become eligible to apply for a loan immediately on
  registration. As set out in `loan_requirements.md`, a member must hold
  active membership and make regular savings contributions for at least
  3 consecutive months before applying for any loan product.

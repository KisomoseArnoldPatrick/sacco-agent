# Minimum Proposal Statement

Our system helps SACCO members prepare a loan application, and helps SACCO relationship officers
review the prepared case. AI is used for retrieval of SACCO policy and product information,
explanation, clarification questions, identifying document types and extracting key details from
uploaded documents, and drafting the case summary. Deterministic software remains responsible
for financial and transaction calculations, product term matching, requirements checking, form
validation, comparison of extracted document details with case data, authorization and audit
logging. The agent may use approved tools (policy and product retrieval, member record and
transaction lookup, product matcher, illustrative repayment calculator, requirements checklist
and form/document validator, case file compiler, and the AI-assisted document reader and summary
drafter) but may not approve or reject loans, recommend lending decisions, score credit, judge a
member's eligibility, suitability or creditworthiness, disburse funds, modify accounts or member
records, perform real transactions, or claim that a document or submitted information is
authentic. We will build and evaluate the system using synthetic member records, transactions,
forms and uploaded documents, and a small corpus of SACCO policy and loan product documents that
the team authors or sources from public material.

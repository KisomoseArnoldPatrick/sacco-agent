# Withdrawal and Exit Policy

## Notice required

- A member wishing to exit Hangs SACCO must submit a written withdrawal
  notice at least **30 days** before their expected payout date.

## Members with an active loan

- A member with an active loan may not withdraw their savings or exit the
  SACCO until the loan is fully repaid, or until the Credit Committee
  approves an alternative arrangement secured by the member's remaining
  guarantors.

## Processing an approved exit

- Once an exit request is approved, the member's share capital and savings
  balance, minus any outstanding obligations, is refunded within **60
  days**.

# Dividends and Profit Sharing

## What earns a dividend

Dividends are declared only on a member's **share capital**, not on their
regular savings or Fixed Deposit Savings balance (Fixed Deposit Savings
instead earns the fixed rate described in `savings_products.md`).

## How dividends are decided

- Dividends are declared once a year at the Annual General Meeting (AGM),
  based on the SACCO's net surplus for the year.
- The dividend rate is not fixed in advance and varies from year to year
  depending on the AGM's decision. This document does not state a specific
  rate because none is fixed by policy.

## How dividends are calculated for a member

- A member's dividend payout is proportional to their average share
  capital held during the financial year.

## Payment

- Approved dividends are credited to the member's account within 30 days
  of the AGM resolution approving them.

# Loan Application Procedure

This document describes the end-to-end steps for a Hangs SACCO loan
application, from initial eligibility check to disbursement.

## Steps

1. **Eligibility check.** The member confirms they meet the general
   eligibility rules in `loan_requirements.md` (active membership and
   savings history).
2. **Product selection.** The member chooses a loan product: Development
   Loan, Emergency Loan or School Fees Loan.
3. **Application form.** The member completes the loan application form,
   stating the amount requested and purpose.
4. **Supporting documents.** The member gathers the documents listed in
   `supporting_documents.md` for the chosen product.
5. **Guarantors.** The member secures the required number of guarantors
   (see `guarantor_requirements.md`) and each guarantor signs a consent
   form in person.
6. **Submission.** The completed application, documents and guarantor
   forms are submitted to a loan officer.
7. **Completeness check.** The loan officer verifies the application is
   complete and the applicant's identity matches SACCO records. Incomplete
   applications are returned to the member with a list of what is missing.
8. **Credit Committee review.** Complete applications are forwarded to the
   Credit Committee, which approves, rejects, or requests additional
   information.
9. **Disbursement.** Approved loans are disbursed within **5 working days**
   of Credit Committee approval, except the Emergency Loan, which is
   disbursed within **48 hours** of approval.
10. **Repayment begins.** The first repayment falls due according to the
    grace period defined in `repayment_policy.md` for the relevant product.

## Who decides what

- The loan officer checks completeness and eligibility; they do not approve
  loans.
- Only the Credit Committee may approve, reject, or set special conditions
  on a loan application.

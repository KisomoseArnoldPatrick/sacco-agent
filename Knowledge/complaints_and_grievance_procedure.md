# Complaints and Grievance Procedure

## Submitting a complaint

- A member submits a written complaint to the SACCO office, describing the
  issue.
- SACCO staff must acknowledge receipt of the complaint within **3 working
  days**.

## Resolution at staff level

- Staff aim to resolve the complaint within **14 days** of acknowledgement.

## Escalation

- If the complaint is not resolved at staff level within 14 days, it is
  escalated to the Supervisory Committee.
- The Supervisory Committee issues a final written decision within **30
  days** of escalation.

## Appealing a loan rejection specifically

- A member whose loan application was rejected may appeal, once, to the
  Credit Committee for reconsideration.
- The appeal must be submitted within **14 days** of the rejection
  decision.

## Decision authority

- Loan officers may not decide complaints or appeals; only the Supervisory
  Committee (general complaints) or the Credit Committee (loan-rejection
  appeals) may issue a final decision (see
  `sacco_governance_and_committees.md`).

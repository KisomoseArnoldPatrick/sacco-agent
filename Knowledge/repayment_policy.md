# Repayment Policy

## Repayment channels

Repayments may be made through any of the following:

- Cash payment at the SACCO office.
- Mobile money transfer to the SACCO's registered pay-in number.
- Payroll deduction, where the member's employer has a check-off agreement
  with the SACCO.

## Grace periods

- Development Loan and School Fees Loan: first repayment due 30 days after
  disbursement.
- Emergency Loan: first repayment due 14 days after disbursement.

## Late payment

- A late payment penalty of **2% of the overdue amount** is charged for
  each month, or part of a month, that a repayment is late.
- A loan with repayments overdue by more than **60 days** is classified as
  in default. Defaulted loans are referred to the Credit Committee and the
  loan's guarantors are formally notified.

## Early repayment

- Members may repay a loan in full before the end of its term at no
  penalty.
- For reducing-balance loans (Development Loan, School Fees Loan), early
  repayment reduces the total interest paid, since interest accrues monthly
  on the outstanding principal.
- For the flat-rate Emergency Loan, early repayment does not reduce the
  total interest due.

## Loan top-ups

- A member may not simply increase an already-approved loan. Any request
  for additional funds must be submitted as a new "top-up" application and
  goes through the full application procedure in
  `loan_application_procedure.md`.

# Member Rights and Responsibilities

## Rights

- To vote at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) on a one-member, one-vote
  basis, regardless of shareholding size.
- To access their own savings, share capital and loan records on request.
- To receive an annual dividend statement, where a dividend is declared
  (see `dividends_and_profit_sharing.md`).
- To lodge a complaint through the process in
  `complaints_and_grievance_procedure.md`.

## Responsibilities

- To maintain the minimum monthly savings contribution described in
  `savings_products.md`.
- To repay any loan according to the agreed schedule in
  `repayment_policy.md`.
- To keep their identification and contact details up to date, as described
  in `member_identification.md`.
- To attend the AGM where reasonably possible, as decisions on dividends
  and governance are made there.

# Loan Application FAQ

**Can I hold two loans at the same time?**
No. A member may hold only one active loan at a time, unless the Credit
Committee grants a specific exception (see `loan_requirements.md`).

**What happens if my guarantor leaves the SACCO?**
You must provide a replacement guarantor within 30 days of the original
guarantor becoming unavailable (see `guarantor_requirements.md`).

**Can I get more money added to a loan I already have?**
No. Additional funds must be requested through a new top-up application,
which goes through the full application procedure (see
`repayment_policy.md` and `loan_application_procedure.md`).

**How long does it take to receive my loan after approval?**
Development Loan and School Fees Loan: within 5 working days of Credit
Committee approval. Emergency Loan: within 48 hours of approval (see
`loan_application_procedure.md`).

**Is there a penalty for paying off my loan early?**
No penalty applies, but the benefit differs by product: reducing-balance
loans (Development Loan, School Fees Loan) save you interest; the flat-rate
Emergency Loan does not (see `repayment_policy.md`).

**Who actually approves my loan?**
The loan officer only checks that your application is complete and your
identity matches SACCO records. Approval, rejection, or special conditions
are decided solely by the Credit Committee (see
`loan_application_procedure.md`).

**Can a family member who is not a SACCO member act as my guarantor?**
No. A guarantor must be an active Hangs SACCO member in good standing (see
`guarantor_requirements.md`).

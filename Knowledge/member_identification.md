# Member Identification

## Membership number

- Every Hangs SACCO member is issued a unique Membership Number at
  registration, in the format `HS-YYYY-NNNNNN` (for example, `HS-2026-000123`).
- The Membership Number is used to identify the member's record in all
  applications, transactions and correspondence.

## Identity verification at registration

New members must complete a Know-Your-Member (KYM) form and present:

1. A valid National ID or passport.
2. One passport-size photograph.

## Identity verification at loan application

Before any loan application is processed, the loan officer must confirm
that the National ID presented matches the identity on record for that
Membership Number. If the two do not match, the application must be placed
on hold pending manual verification by the Membership Office.

## Updating member details

- Changes to a member's name, phone number or next of kin must be submitted
  in writing and verified against the original National ID before the
  membership record is updated.

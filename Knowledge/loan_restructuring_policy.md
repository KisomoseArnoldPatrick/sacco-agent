# Loan Restructuring Policy

## When restructuring may be requested

- A member experiencing genuine financial hardship may apply in writing to
  restructure their loan (for example, an extended repayment term or a
  revised schedule) **before** the loan falls into default as defined in
  `repayment_policy.md`.

## Review and approval

- Restructuring requests are reviewed and decided **only** by the Credit
  Committee. Loan officers cannot approve a restructuring request (see
  `sacco_governance_and_committees.md`).

## Limits

- A given loan may be restructured **only once** during its term.
- Restructuring changes the future repayment schedule but does not remove
  or waive any late payment penalties that have already accrued under
  `repayment_policy.md`.

## Relationship to default and recovery

- A loan that has already been classified as in default follows the
  process in `loan_default_and_recovery.md` instead of this restructuring
  process.

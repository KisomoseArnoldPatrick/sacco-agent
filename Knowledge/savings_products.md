# Savings Products

Hangs SACCO offers two savings products. A member's savings balance is used
to determine their loan ceiling, as described in `loan_requirements.md`.

## Regular Savings Account

- Minimum monthly contribution: **UGX 20,000**.
- Funds may be withdrawn subject to the notice and conditions in
  `withdrawal_and_exit_policy.md`.
- Regular savings balances count toward a member's loan ceiling but do not
  themselves earn a fixed interest rate; they may instead be eligible for an
  annual dividend as described in `dividends_and_profit_sharing.md`.

## Fixed Deposit Savings

- Minimum deposit: **UGX 500,000**.
- Funds are locked for a minimum term of **6 months**.
- Earns a fixed interest rate of **6% per annum**, paid at maturity.
- Fixed Deposit balances also count toward a member's loan ceiling.

## Share Capital

- Share capital is separate from savings and is the amount used to
  calculate annual dividends (see `dividends_and_profit_sharing.md`). The
  minimum initial share capital is set at registration (see
  `membership_registration.md`).

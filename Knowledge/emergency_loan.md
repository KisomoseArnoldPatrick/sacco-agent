# Emergency Loan

## Purpose

The Emergency Loan provides fast access to funds for urgent, unforeseen
needs such as medical treatment or bereavement.

## Terms

| Feature               | Detail                                                           |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maximum amount        | The lower of UGX 2,000,000 or 3 times the member's total savings |
| Interest rate         | 10% per annum, flat rate                                         |
| Repayment period      | Up to 6 months                                                   |
| Guarantors required   | 1 (see `guarantor_requirements.md`)                              |
| Grace period          | First repayment due 14 days after disbursement                   |
| Disbursement timeline | Within 48 hours of Credit Committee approval                     |

## Additional requirements

- Proof of the emergency (for example, a hospital bill or death
  certificate) is encouraged but not mandatory for processing, as noted in
  `supporting_documents.md`.

## Notes

- Because the interest rate is flat, the total interest due does not
  reduce if the member repays the loan early.

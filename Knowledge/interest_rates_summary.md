# Interest Rate Summary

This is a consolidated reference of interest rates across Hangs SACCO
products. In case of any discrepancy, the individual product document is
authoritative.

## Loan products

| Product           | Interest rate                | Basis            |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| Development Loan   | 12% per annum                 | Reducing balance  |
| School Fees Loan    | 11% per annum                 | Reducing balance  |
| Emergency Loan       | 10% per annum                 | Flat rate         |

Full terms for each are in `development_loan.md`, `school_fees_loan.md` and
`emergency_loan.md`.

## Savings products

| Product              | Rate                          |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Fixed Deposit Savings  | 6% per annum, paid at maturity |
| Regular Savings         | No fixed rate; eligible for annual dividend (see `dividends_and_profit_sharing.md`) |

## Penalties

- Late loan repayment penalty: 2% of the overdue amount per month or part
  month (see `repayment_policy.md`).

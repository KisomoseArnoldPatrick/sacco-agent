# SACCO Governance and Committees

This document sets out who holds decision-making authority at Hangs SACCO.
It directly supports the project's AI Boundary Matrix: the AI assistant may
explain policy and help prepare a case, deterministic software may
calculate figures such as repayment schedules, but only the human bodies
listed below may make binding decisions.

## Board of Directors

- Elected by members at the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
- Sets overall SACCO policy and strategic direction.
- Approves new membership applications (see `membership_registration.md`).
- Makes the final decision on escalated loan-default recovery action (see
  `loan_default_and_recovery.md`).

## Credit Committee

- The sole authority that may approve, reject, or set conditions on a loan
  application (see `loan_application_procedure.md`).
- The sole authority that may approve a loan restructuring request (see
  `loan_restructuring_policy.md`).
- Reviews defaulted loans at Step 2 of the recovery process (see
  `loan_default_and_recovery.md`).
- Decides loan-rejection appeals (see
  `complaints_and_grievance_procedure.md`).

## Supervisory Committee

- Handles escalated member complaints that are not loan-rejection appeals
  (see `complaints_and_grievance_procedure.md`).
- Provides internal oversight of SACCO operations.

## Loan Officers

- Front-line staff who check that applications are complete and that the
  applicant's identity matches SACCO records (see
  `member_identification.md` and `loan_application_procedure.md`).
- Loan officers do not have authority to approve, reject, restructure, or
  decide complaints. Their role is limited to verification and preparing a
  case for Committee review.

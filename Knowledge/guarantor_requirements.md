# Guarantor Requirements

Guarantors provide assurance to Hangs SACCO that a loan will be repaid. The
following rules apply to all guarantors.

## Who may act as a guarantor

- Must be an active Hangs SACCO member in good standing.
- Must not currently be guaranteeing more than **2** other active loans.
- Combined, the guarantor(s) on an application must hold savings equal to at
  least **20% of the loan amount** being guaranteed.
- A member may not guarantee their own loan application.
- The guarantor must sign the guarantor consent form in person at the SACCO
  office.

## Number of guarantors required, by loan product

| Loan product     | Guarantors required |
| ---------------- | ------------------- |
| Development Loan | 2                   |
| School Fees Loan | 2                   |
| Emergency Loan   | 1                   |

## If a guarantor becomes unavailable

- If a guarantor exits the SACCO or is otherwise unable to continue as
  guarantor after a loan has been disbursed, the borrowing member must
  provide a replacement guarantor within **30 days**.
- Failure to provide a replacement guarantor within this period may result
  in the loan being referred to the Credit Committee for review.

# Development Loan

## Purpose

The Development Loan supports members undertaking income-generating or
personal development activities, such as small business expansion, land
improvement, or asset purchase.

## Terms

| Feature             | Detail                                                                      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maximum amount      | UGX 10,000,000, subject to the 3x-savings ceiling in `loan_requirements.md` |
| Interest rate       | 12% per annum, reducing balance                                             |
| Repayment period    | Up to 24 months                                                             |
| Guarantors required | 2 (see `guarantor_requirements.md`)                                         |
| Grace period        | First repayment due 30 days after disbursement                              |

## Additional requirements

- A short written statement describing the business or development purpose
  the loan will fund must accompany the application (see
  `supporting_documents.md`).

## Notes

- Interest is calculated monthly on the outstanding principal (reducing
  balance), so early repayment reduces total interest paid.

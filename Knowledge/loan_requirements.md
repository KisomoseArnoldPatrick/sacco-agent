# General Loan Requirements

This document sets out the baseline eligibility rules that apply to every loan
product offered by Hangs SACCO, unless a specific product document states a
different rule.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a registered member of Hangs SACCO in good standing.
- The applicant must have held active membership for at least **3 consecutive
  months** before applying.
- The applicant must have made regular savings contributions for at least
  **3 consecutive months** immediately before the application.
- The applicant must not have any loan currently classified as in default.
- A member may hold only **one active loan at a time**, unless the Credit
  Committee grants a specific exception.

## Loan amount ceiling

- Unless a specific loan product states otherwise, the maximum loan amount is
  **three (3) times the member's total accumulated savings** at the time of
  application.

## General application requirements

- Completed loan application form.
- Supporting documents as listed in `supporting_documents.md`.
- Guarantors as specified in `guarantor_requirements.md` for the relevant
  loan product.

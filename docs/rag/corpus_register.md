documents**, written for this project. No real member data, real SACCO
records, or externally sourced/copyrighted material is used. This satisfies
the assignment's "public, team-created, synthetic or otherwise authorized
data" requirement (Section 2).

## Notes on coverage and known gaps

The corpus deliberately does **not** cover certain topics, so the RAG
evaluation can test the system's "unsupported-answer handling" (Week 3
requirement). Known gaps include: mortgage/home loan products, foreign
currency or cross-border transactions, credit scoring methodology, and
inter-SACCO membership transfers. Any question touching these topics should
be answered as unsupported rather than guessed.

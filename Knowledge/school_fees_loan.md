# School Fees Loan

## Purpose

The School Fees Loan helps members cover tuition and related education
costs for themselves or their dependents.

## Terms

| Feature             | Detail                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maximum amount      | UGX 5,000,000, subject to the 3x-savings ceiling in `loan_requirements.md`                    |
| Interest rate       | 11% per annum, reducing balance                                                               |
| Repayment period    | Up to 4 months if aligned to a single school term, or up to 12 months if covering annual fees |
| Guarantors required | 2 (see `guarantor_requirements.md`)                                                           |
| Grace period        | First repayment due 30 days after disbursement                                                |

## Additional requirements

- An official fee structure or invoice from the learning institution must
  be submitted, showing the amount due and the term or year it covers (see
  `supporting_documents.md`).

## Notes

- Disbursement is made directly to the member's SACCO account; the SACCO
  does not pay institutions directly on the member's behalf.
